# Frozen protocol for finite switching forecasts

Locally fixed before evaluation on 16 November 2026. The model comes from Catsis, Hall and Jeffrey, *Perturbation and determinacy of nonsmooth systems* (2025), Fig. 2 and Appendix A. The target is the simultaneous-mode occupancy of a generic two-threshold dynamical system. We examine a finite-window forecast, a distinct problem from their asymptotic analysis. [Author manuscript](https://nonsmooth.land/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2024_paradigm.pdf).

## Estimand and controls

The four mode fields use the sign convention `(+a0,+b0),(-a1,+b1),(+a2,-b2),(-a3,-b3)`. At most the switching law and its known scale vary. Neither comparison point forecaster receives trajectories or regularisation labels. Baselines are the product closure with both mean drifts zero, and the maximum-entropy occupancy distribution with both mean drifts zero. The alternative forecast is the sharp interval obtained from the mode-displacement identity, known initial state and a deterministic bound on the terminal state for each kernel. A universal envelope takes the largest of those bounds over all admissible kernels. We audit its coverage for mode 11 and for both switch marginals. The separate interval `[0,1]` is the unconditional coverage control.

Use the three published coefficient sets, 24 prospectively seeded coupled fields, and 16 uncoupled fields with analytically determined marginal occupancies. Each test cell uses all three regularisations: direct Euler sign, Euler hysteresis and Euler tanh weights. Cross step sizes .10 and .04, scale multipliers .5 and 2, and two initial states drawn from independent fixed-seed uniform distributions on [-.18,.18]. Use time horizon 32. Simultaneous occupancy is the actual indicator in the two threshold kernels and the product of smooth weights in the tanh kernel. Median absolute errors, widths and kernel ranges across matched settings are recorded separately for each field family.

Primary success requires audited coverage of all simulated mode-11 values and a tightening of at least .20 in median interval width versus the unconditional interval for the independent generated coupled family. Report misses and widths. Point forecast accuracy and cross-kernel disagreement are supporting comparisons. The exact integrated balance identity is checked against all field and probe trajectories, including simultaneous changes in the two coordinates. No data-dependent parameter fitting or changes to the gate.
